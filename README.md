# Roast the Smart Fridge: One Line, Best Laugh Wins

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge.svg)](https://make.fun/c/u-kiencx-roast-the-smart-fridge-one-line-best-laugh-b354d6c9)

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[u-kiencx-roast-the-smart-fridge-one-line-best-laugh-b354d6c9](https://make.fun/c/u-kiencx-roast-the-smart-fridge-one-line-best-laugh-b354d6c9).

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes one original 153-character roast to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> My smart fridge knows I eat ice cream at 2 a.m. and tells my phone, my
> watch, and probably my mother — the only witness here with Wi-Fi and zero
> loyalty.

The angle is the fridge as a snitch: it tracks a real behaviour (late-night
eating, synced to phone and watch) and the sting lands on loyalty, not on a
stock "orders too much milk" joke.

Run it with `python3 main.py` — it writes `/output/entry.txt` and asserts
the line is a single line of 1–160 characters.
